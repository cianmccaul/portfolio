Featured work

The project I'm proudest of, in use at a real clinic.

Clinic platform

Online booking, payments and a staff hub for BodyMed Healthcare, Clontarf

Patients could only book by phone or email, late cancellations and no-shows cost therapists money, and reception tracked payments by hand.

I built one system on top of the clinic's diary (Cliniko) and payments (Stripe). Patients book and pay in a couple of minutes. Reception handles refunds, fees and phone bookings from one dashboard, and each therapist is paid straight into their own Stripe account.

How a booking works

  1. Patient picks a timeTreatment, therapist and slot, on any phone
  2. Slot is heldRe-checked against the diary and held for 30 minutes
  3. Pays securelyStripe Checkout, card saved for late cancellations
  4. Booked in the diaryAppointment created in Cliniko, confirmation emailed
  5. Reception sees itOn the dashboard with payment, notes and actions
If it failsSlot taken while they paid? They keep the payment and pick another time. Diary down? A full refund, automatically, and the clinic gets an alert. A reconciliation check runs every morning.
