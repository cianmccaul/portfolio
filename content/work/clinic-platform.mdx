## Context

BodyMed Healthcare is a clinic in Clontarf with several therapists. Before this, a patient who wanted an appointment rang or emailed. Late cancellations and no-shows cost therapists money, and reception kept track of payments by hand.

The clinic’s diary is Cliniko, a practice management system. I built on top of it, with Stripe for payments, instead of replacing it.

A few things shaped the design:

- **The therapists are self-employed.** A payment for an appointment with them is their money, not the clinic’s.
- **People still work in the diary directly.** Reception can book, move or cancel an appointment in Cliniko at any moment, including while a patient is halfway through paying online.
- **Some treatments share equipment.** Two therapists can’t use the same machine at the same time.
- **The clinic has to be able to run it without me.** Every member of staff has a help centre with step-by-step guides.

The production code is private because it’s tied to the clinic’s accounts. The [public demo](https://cian-clinic-demo.vercel.app) is the production interface running as a fictional clinic, Northbank Physio, with a pretend back end in the browser.

Before launch I wrote the requirements, a failure modes and effects analysis of everything that could go wrong with money and bookings, a test plan, a go/no-go checklist, a runbook, and an architecture decision record for each major choice.

## Decisions

<Decision title="Take payment, then book" rejected="Authorise the card first and capture later">
  Card authorisations expire after about seven days, and many bookings are further out than that. Authorise-then-capture would have needed a second flow anyway, so I chose to capture, then book, with automatic compensation when booking fails, plus monitoring.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Ask the diary every time" rejected="Keep the platform’s own copy of the diary">
  The platform never stores who is booked when. It asks Cliniko each time, so there is one source of truth. Because people can still change things directly in the diary or in Stripe, a reconciliation job runs every morning and emails a list of anything that doesn’t match, only when something needs a human.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Keep the payment when a slot is taken mid-payment" rejected="Refund straight away">
  Until 3 November 2026 a patient whose slot was taken while they paid was refunded at once. Patients preferred not having to pay twice. Now the payment is kept and they get a link to pick another time with the same therapist, or to ask for a refund. If they do neither within 7 days, they are refunded in full automatically.
</Decision>

<Decision title="One module owns the cancellation policy" rejected="Wording the 24-hour rule on each screen">
  One module holds the rule and its exact wording. The booking screens, “Manage my booking”, reception, the emails and the policy page all use it, so a patient never reads two versions of the rule.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Passkeys for anyone who can move money" rejected="An emailed code alone">
  Passkeys (Face ID or a fingerprint) are required for anyone who can move money. An emailed code alone only opens the guides. A new device has to be approved from one that’s already trusted, and roles are enforced on the server and unit-tested.
</Decision>

<Decision title="The confirmation page polls for the truth" rejected="Assuming the booking succeeded once the card is charged">
  Booking happens in a webhook after payment, so the page asks for the booking’s status and shows what actually happened.
</Decision>

## Hardest problem

The hardest problem in an online booking system is a patient who has **paid but isn’t booked**, or is **booked twice**. Payment and booking live in two different systems, and either can fail, or be retried, between the two steps.

The flow is built so neither can happen:

## Results

- **Live since October 2026**, taking real bookings and payments every day at [book.bodymed.ie](https://book.bodymed.ie).
- The demo’s end-to-end tests check that a repeated webhook gives one appointment and one charge, that a slot taken mid-payment keeps the payment, and that an unused rebook is refunded after 7 days.
- Type checking, unit tests and a production build run on every push.
