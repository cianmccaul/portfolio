# Design System: Cian McCaul

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Broadsheet Résumé"**

The site is set like a newspaper's front page. The name is a masthead cut to the width of the column. A full-strength rule sits under it, and the facts are laid out as a spec sheet of labels and values on hairlines, not as chips or cards. One self-hosted typeface, a cool 12-step neutral and a single deep teal carry everything. Colour is kept for meaning: the accent says "this goes somewhere", and status colours appear only in data.

There is one dramatic surface, the full-bleed **night band** on the home page that presents the BodyMed Hub. A soft three-colour **aurora** sits behind each masthead, and glass is reserved for floating chrome. Everything else is flat and quiet, with generous space. Motion is short (300 ms ceiling) and turns off with reduced motion. The theme follows the system, can be forced light or dark, and every colour is defined for both modes at once with `light-dark()`.

The system is closed by construction. Tailwind's defaults are switched off in `src/styles/tokens.css`, so a size, colour, gap, radius or shadow that isn't a token doesn't exist as a class.

The language in this file comes from the project's own source comments and planning docs (`src/styles/*.css`, `docs/build-spec.md`, `docs/glass.md`), not from a new interview.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A masthead name sized to its column, over a full-strength ink rule.
- Facts shown as a spec sheet on hairlines, never as tag chips.
- One family (Inter Variable), three weights (400, 500, 600), a fluid scale of about 1.25.
- A cool neutral (hue 250) with one deep teal accent (hue 200), in light and dark.
- Flat by default: shadows only on product pictures and floating chrome, and none in dark mode.
- Glass on the nav, ⌘K palette, appearance panel and toasts only, in three tiers that each work on their own.
- Product screenshots stay light in both themes, as on a product page.

## Colors

A cool, near-colourless neutral with one deep teal that always means action, plus a dark band and an aurora that use the same family of teal, blue and green.

### Primary
- **Deep Harbour Teal** (`accent`): the primary button, links, focus rings, text selection (at 25%) and code keywords. Nothing decorative. In light mode it sits at L 0.52 (5.2:1 on white); dark mode lifts and desaturates it.
- **Harbour Teal, Pressed** (`accent-strong`): hover and press state of the accent.
- **Accent Ink** (`accent-ink`): text on the accent: white in light mode, near-black in dark.

### Neutral
A 12-step scale, `n1`–`n12`, hue 250, in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Components use the role names below, never the raw steps.
- **Paper** (`canvas`, n1): the page background.
- **Sheet White** (`surface`): cards, secondary buttons, solid chrome.
- **Inset Grey** (`well`, `well-strong`): insets, stages, mats around pictures, code blocks; `well-strong` is a well when hovered.
- **Hairline** (`line`, n5): dividers inside groups and around mats.
- **Control Edge** (`line-strong`, n7): control borders and the rules of the spec sheet and proof strip.
- **Newsprint Ink** (`ink`, n12): body text and the rule under each masthead.
- **Pencil** (`muted`, n11): secondary text, spec labels, list markers. Under `prefers-contrast: more` it becomes ink.

### Status (data only)
- **Signal Green** (`ok`, `ok-soft`) and **Amber** (`warn`, `warn-soft`): the dashboard previews and draft-build TODO markers. Never used for decoration or for the brand.

### The night band and the aurora
- **Night** (`night`, oklch 0.17 0.02 232): the one full-bleed dark surface, the same in both themes. It sets `color-scheme: dark`, so everything inside uses the dark half of the tokens.
- **Night glows** (`night-teal`, `night-blue`, `night-green`): three radial lights inside the band.
- **Aurora** (`aurora-teal`, `aurora-blue`, `aurora-green`): three soft radial lights behind a masthead, fading into the canvas through a mask, with 5% grain to stop banding. Teal, blue and green, never purple. Pale in light mode, a dim glow in dark.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Teal is for the primary action, links, focus and selection. If it isn't one of those, it isn't teal.

**The Never-Purple Rule.** The aurora and the night band use teal, blue and green only.

**The Roles-Not-Steps Rule.** Components use `canvas`, `surface`, `well`, `line`, `ink`, `muted` and so on, never `n1`–`n12` directly.

## Typography

**Display and body font:** Inter Variable, self-hosted as one 48 KB Latin subset (weights 400–600), with a metric-matched Arial fallback ("Inter Fallback") so the swap moves nothing.
**Mono font:** the system's monospace, only for code, metrics and `<kbd>`.

**Character:** one configured grotesque doing every job, through size, tracking and weight rather than a second family. Body text turns on `ss03` (round quotes and commas) and `cv05` (a tailed lowercase l, so I, l and 1 can't be confused). Headings keep the plain l.

### Hierarchy
- **Masthead** (600, the column width ÷ 5.17 em, 0.86, −0.055em): the name on the home page, sized to its container with `cqw`, never wrapping, with the C pulled back onto the rule's edge.
- **Hero** (600, 52→120 px, 0.95, −0.045em): broadsheet headings: the clinic band's title and inner pages' mastheads.
- **Figure** (600, 48→96 px, 1, −0.04em): reserved for owner-approved metrics. Nothing uses it yet.
- **Display** (600, 40→72 px, 1, −0.035em): large page titles.
- **H1** (600, 32→48 px, 1.08, −0.025em): section titles site-wide and the hero's headline line.
- **H2** (600, 24→32 px, 1.15, −0.015em): case-study section headings, each with an ink rule above.
- **H3** (500, 19→22 px, 1.3, −0.01em): item titles; also proof-strip figures.
- **Body large** (400, 18→20 px, 1.55): ledes.
- **Body** (400, 16→17 px, 1.6): running text, capped at 65ch in prose.
- **Small** (14 px, 1.5): buttons, nav, spec labels, captions under work.
- **Caption** (500, 12→13 px, 1.4, +0.02em): `<kbd>`, draft markers, fine labels.

Every size is fluid between 360 px and 1280 px viewports.

### Named Rules
**The Three Weights Rule.** 400, 500 and 600 only. Nothing lighter than 400, nothing heavier than 600.

**The Colour-Not-Weight Rule.** Hover changes colour or opacity, never weight, so nothing shifts.

**The Typographer's Marks Rule.** Real quotes and dashes (’ “ ” – —), `text-wrap: balance` on headings, `pretty` on paragraphs, tabular figures for numbers.

## Layout

A 1200 px page container (`max-w-page`) with a fluid gutter (16→48 px), on a 12-column grid at desktop widths. Sections are separated by a fluid gap (64→160 px), and each section title sits under a rule. Every gap comes from a 4/8 scale named by pixel value (`gap-24` is 24 px); nothing off the scale compiles.

- **Measures:** prose 65ch; the links card 460 px; the case-study dashboard 640 px; the CV sheet 800 px; the phone in the clinic band 280 px.
- **First screen:** the home hero fits on the first screen at 1280×800, 1440×900, 390×844 and 360×740. On a phone the order is headline, actions, spec sheet, proof row, then the paragraph.
- **Rhythm on the home page:** scroll snapping is proximity only, never mandatory. The hero snaps to its start, each work item to its centre, and the clinic band to its top edge. It turns off under reduced motion and while Back restores a position.
- **Breakpoints:** Tailwind's defaults (sm 640, md 768, lg 1024, xl 1280). Designs are checked at 360, 768, 1280 and 1600.
- **Anchors** clear the floating nav with a 96 px scroll padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default, using tonal layering (`canvas` → `surface` → `well`) and hairlines. Shadows are for things that float or are pictures: product screenshots and drawn dashboards, the phone, and glass chrome. Dark mode drops every shadow token to transparent and relies on hairlines and lighter surfaces.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **e1** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.06)`): the faintest lift, for small inset items.
- **e2** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -4px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.12)`): product pictures and drawn dashboards; solid-tier chrome.
- **e3** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -12px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.2)`): panels (the ⌘K palette and the appearance panel).
- **Phone** (`0 0 0 1px oklch(1 0 0 / 0.14), 0 32px 64px -24px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.55)`): the device frame only.

### Glass
Glass (`src/styles/skin.css`, rules in `docs/glass.md`) is a material for floating chrome. It has three tiers, each complete on its own:
1. **Solid:** opaque surface, hairline, e2. Used when Reduce glass is on, when the system asks for reduced transparency or more contrast, in forced colours, and without `backdrop-filter`.
2. **Frosted:** a tint over `blur() saturate(170%)`, with a specular top edge, a hairline, a soft dark rim and a shadow. Blur grows with elevation: 12 px toasts, 20 px nav, 32 px panels.
3. **Refracted:** Chromium only, nav pill only. An SVG displacement filter bends the backdrop at the rim.

The tint is heavy enough that text keeps at least 4.5:1 over pure black or pure white beneath it.

### Named Rules
**The Chrome-Only Glass Rule.** Glass goes on the nav, ⌘K, the appearance panel, toasts and the small clear note in the night band. Never behind body text. At most two glass surfaces on screen at rest.

**The Pictures-Float Rule.** Only pictures and chrome cast shadows. Text blocks and sections never do.

## Shapes

Soft, concentric corners. Nested corners follow outer radius = inner radius + padding, as on the mat (`radius-sm + mat padding`) and the phone screen (`radius-device − bezel`).

- **xs (6px):** `<kbd>`, inputs, inline code, and the focus ring of shapeless elements (text links, list rows).
- **sm (12px):** buttons, chips, screenshots and media inside cards.
- **md (20px):** cards and portrait photos.
- **lg (28px):** the nav pill and dialogs.
- **pill (999px):** nav items and toasts.
- **device (40px):** the phone frame.

Rules are straight and full-width: a 1 px ink rule under mastheads and above case-study H2s, `line-strong` rules in spec sheets and the proof strip.

## Components

### Buttons
Calm, compact and tactile.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (12px), at least 44 px tall, 20 px horizontal padding, small text at 500.
- **Primary:** teal fill with accent ink. One per group; on the hero it is "View CV".
- **Secondary:** surface fill with a `line-strong` border and ink text.
- **Hover / press:** hover changes background colour only (120 ms) and is gated to `(hover: hover)`; press scales to 0.97. A transparent border keeps an edge in forced-colours mode.

### Links
- **Inline/action links:** teal, 500, underline fades in on hover. A standalone link gets a full 44 px touch target (`.link-action`).
- **Links in prose** stay underlined at rest, since colour alone isn't enough.

### Spec sheet
The site's signature way of showing facts: a definition list of label and value rows on `line-strong` hairlines, with the label column fixed at 7.5em. Labels are muted small text; values are 500 weight. There is a compact cut for tight spaces. It replaces chips and tags for logistics and project facts.

### Proof strip
Three cells between `line-strong` rules, separated by hairlines. Each is a link to the section that backs it up: a figure at H3 size in tabular numerals, a muted unit, and (from tablet up) a muted label with a teal arrow. On a phone it is figures only.

### Navigation
- **Style:** one floating glass pill (radius 28px, 4 px padding) sticky 16 px from the top, centred. It holds the monogram (home), the text items, and, once JavaScript has run, Search (⌘K/Ctrl K, an icon on phones) and Appearance.
- **Items:** pill-shaped, 44 px tall, small text at 500. The current page and hover/press show a 9% ink wash.
- **Over the night band** the pill takes the dark tint.

### Mat
A neutral board one step off the page (`well`, hairline inset) around product screenshots with strong colours of their own and around photos, so they read as pictures set into the page.

### Night band (signature)
The one full-bleed dark section on the home page: the BodyMed Hub, with a phone frame, three glows and grain. It forces the dark half of every token, while product pictures inside it stay light (`.scheme-light`).

### Draft markers
Amber dashed boxes for `TODO` content and "Pending approval" facts, only in draft builds, never in production or print.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every size, gap, colour, radius and shadow from `src/styles/tokens.css`; add a token before using a new value.
- **Do** keep the accent for the primary action, links, focus and selection.
- **Do** show facts as a spec sheet on hairlines.
- **Do** keep touch targets at least 44 px (`spacing-tap`).
- **Do** keep UI motion at 300 ms or less, using `--dur-*` and `--ease-*`, so reduced motion can retune it in one place.
- **Do** give every glass surface its solid fallback, and use the panel tint if it needs muted text.
- **Do** keep product screenshots and drawn dashboards light in both themes, on a mat when their own colours are strong.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use purple, gradient text, or a glow orb as decoration; the aurora is teal, blue and green, behind mastheads only.
- **Don't** put glass behind body text, or put muted or accent-coloured text on the nav pill.
- **Don't** use a font weight outside 400–600, or change weight on hover.
- **Don't** box everything in cards; cards are only for things that are clickable.
- **Don't** use status colours outside data and draft markers.
- **Don't** add shadows in dark mode or to text sections.
- **Don't** use `n1`–`n12` directly in components.
